Privacy Policy

Overview

The John Trust respects your privacy. This policy explains the information we collect through John Trust Open Publishing, why we collect it, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

When you subscribe to publishing updates, we collect the email address you provide and your consent to receive those communications. Our website may also collect limited technical information needed for security, reliability and basic site operation.

How We Use Information

We use your information to provide the services you request, send publishing news and updates you have chosen to receive, maintain the security and operation of the website, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Newsletter & Consent

We send newsletter communications only when you choose to subscribe. You may withdraw your consent and unsubscribe at any time by using the unsubscribe option included in our communications.

Third-Party Services

John Trust Open Publishing may link to or use third-party services, including GitHub and YouTube. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and practices.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers when necessary to operate the website or deliver services you request, or when law requires us to do so.

Your Choices

You may unsubscribe from communications at any time. You may also contact The John Trust with questions or requests concerning personal information associated with the Open Publishing service.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please contact The John Trust through the Contact Us page on johntrust.org.
